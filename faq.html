§ FAQ

Frequently asked

Everything you need to know about ordering, materials, personalisation, and delivery. Can't find your answer? Reach out via WhatsApp.

01

How do I place an order?

Browse the catalogue and check out online, or WhatsApp us to discuss a custom piece. Once your payment is confirmed through Billplz, your order enters our production queue and we'll reach out to confirm details.

02

Can I personalise my item with names or text?

Yes. Add your personalisation — names, dates, short phrases — in the order notes at checkout. We'll confirm the exact wording with you via WhatsApp before production starts, since every piece is made to order.

03

What materials do you cut and engrave?

Our core materials are plywood and acrylic, precision laser-cut and engraved for a clean, durable finish. As they're natural/manufactured materials, slight variation in grain or tone between pieces is normal.

04

What is the typical production time?

Nothing ships from stock — every piece is cut and engraved after your order is confirmed. Standard production typically takes a few days depending on the piece and current order volume; rush production is available for an additional charge if you need it sooner.

05

How is my order shipped?

We ship anywhere in Malaysia via Poslaju or J&T Express — you choose at checkout with live rates for your address. Estimated transit is 1–2 working days within Johor, 2–3 days elsewhere in West Malaysia, and 3–5 days to Sabah, Sarawak, or Labuan, after production is complete.

06

Can I track my order?

Yes — use your order number on our Track Order page to follow both the production stage and live courier tracking once it ships.

07

What is your return policy?

Because every item is made specifically for you, we're generally unable to accept returns or exchanges for change-of-mind once production has started. If your order arrives damaged, defective, or different from what was confirmed, WhatsApp us within 3 days of delivery with photos and we'll make it right. Full details on our Shipping & Returns page.

08

Do you take bulk or corporate orders?

Yes — wedding favours, Hari Guru gifts, and corporate/event batches are a specialty. Share your quantities, materials, and deadline via WhatsApp and we'll send a tailored quote.

09

How do I care for my piece?

Wood/plywood: wipe with a dry cloth, avoid prolonged moisture. Acrylic: clean with a microfiber cloth, avoid solvents and abrasive cleaners.
